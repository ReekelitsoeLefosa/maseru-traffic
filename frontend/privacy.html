Maseru Smart Traffic · Last updated: [date you publish]

This app shows live traffic at junctions in Maseru, suggests faster routes and can alert you when your trip gets congested. It is operated by [your name or organisation] (“we”). Contact: [your email address].

What we collect, and why

  • Phone number – only if you sign up for SMS or WhatsApp alerts, to send them. We confirm the number with a one-time code.
  • Your alert choices – your daily trip (start and end junction) and the junctions you watch, to decide which alerts to send you.
  • Notification token – if you turn on alerts in the app, your phone gives us an anonymous token so we can send notifications to it. It does not contain your name or number.
  • Messages to our WhatsApp bot – used to answer you, and kept in a log for troubleshooting.

Location

“Start from the junction nearest me” uses your location on your phone only, to pick the nearest junction. Your location is not sent to us or stored.

Traffic cameras

Cameras at junctions are analysed automatically to count vehicles by type (car, 4+1 cab, minibus taxi, bus, truck, motorcycle). We store counts, not video. The app shows a recent, low-resolution snapshot from each camera; snapshots are not kept. We do not read number plates or identify people.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services that deliver messages for us: [e.g. Twilio or Meta for SMS/WhatsApp, Google Firebase for app notifications], and only what they need to deliver each message.

How long we keep it

Your phone number and alert choices are kept until you stop alerts. Message logs are kept for up to [e.g. 90 days]. Traffic counts contain no personal data and may be kept for planning.

Stopping alerts and deleting your data

  • App notifications: open Alerts and tap Turn off – your token and choices are deleted.
  • SMS: reply STOP. WhatsApp: send stop to the bot.
  • To have everything about you deleted, email [your email address].

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
